About Us

Solid Work From the Ground Up

We are a local building firm based at Riverside. Most of what we do starts below ground level: digging out, getting the footings right, and putting up walls and extensions that stay where they are put. If you have a job in mind, give us a ring and we will come and take a look.

The parts of a build you never see are the parts that matter most. Footings, drainage and steelwork are where we start, and where we take our time.
